# AI Adoption Roadmap

## Overview

Successful AI adoption requires more than selecting an AI platform.

Organisations should establish the appropriate governance, security, data and capability foundations while progressively identifying and scaling valuable use cases.

The objective is to create sustainable AI adoption rather than isolated experimentation.

---

## Adoption Model

A practical AI adoption journey can be structured into five stages:

**Discover → Govern → Experiment → Scale → Optimise**

---

## Stage 1 — Discover

### Objective

Understand how AI could create value across the organisation.

### Activities

- Identify potential use cases
- Engage business stakeholders
- Understand existing AI usage
- Identify productivity opportunities
- Assess current capability
- Identify key risks
- Establish executive sponsorship

### Outputs

- AI opportunity register
- Initial AI use cases
- Initial risk assessment
- Executive priorities

---

## Stage 2 — Govern

### Objective

Establish the minimum governance required to enable responsible experimentation.

### Activities

- Develop AI policy
- Establish acceptable-use requirements
- Define AI risk tiers
- Establish approval processes
- Define data handling requirements
- Establish security requirements
- Identify approved AI platforms

### Outputs

- AI governance framework
- AI policy
- Risk assessment process
- Approved platforms
- Governance responsibilities

---

## Stage 3 — Experiment

### Objective

Test AI use cases in controlled environments.

### Activities

- Select priority use cases
- Conduct pilots
- Measure productivity and business outcomes
- Gather user feedback
- Assess security and privacy
- Validate AI outputs
- Document lessons learned

### Example Use Cases

- Document summarisation
- Meeting assistance
- Knowledge search
- Customer service support
- Software development assistance
- Data analysis
- Workflow automation

---

## Stage 4 — Scale

### Objective

Expand successful AI use cases across the organisation.

### Activities

- Establish production architecture
- Integrate AI with business systems
- Implement monitoring
- Train employees
- Establish support processes
- Formalise ownership
- Measure benefits
- Review risk

### Key Consideration

Not every successful pilot should automatically become an enterprise solution.

Scaling decisions should consider:

**Value + Risk + Cost + Security + User Adoption**

---

## Stage 5 — Optimise

### Objective

Move from individual AI tools toward an integrated AI capability.

### Activities

- Measure enterprise adoption
- Optimise AI spend
- Consolidate platforms
- Improve governance
- Automate workflows
- Integrate AI into business processes
- Monitor emerging technology
- Review the AI portfolio

---

## AI Use Case Prioritisation

Use cases can be prioritised based on:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| **Value** | What business benefit could be achieved? |
| **Feasibility** | Can the organisation realistically implement it? |
| **Risk** | What could go wrong? |
| **Data** | Is the required data available and appropriate? |
| **Adoption** | Will users actually use it? |
| **Cost** | What investment is required? |

---

## Example Prioritisation

### High Value / Low Risk

Prioritise for rapid experimentation.

Examples:

- Internal productivity
- Document summarisation
- Knowledge management
- Meeting assistance

### High Value / High Risk

Require formal governance and executive sponsorship.

Examples:

- Customer decision support
- Financial decision support
- Employee-impacting AI
- Automated customer interactions

### Low Value / High Risk

Generally avoid unless there is a compelling reason.

---

## Adoption Enablers

Successful adoption requires more than technology.

Key enablers include:

- Executive sponsorship
- Clear governance
- User education
- Change management
- Data quality
- Security
- Privacy
- Architecture
- Vendor management
- Measurement

---

## AI Operating Model

As adoption matures, organisations should establish clear ownership across:

**Business + Technology + Security + Risk + Legal + Data**

A central AI governance function may coordinate standards while business teams remain accountable for their individual use cases.

---

## Success Measures

AI adoption should be measured using both adoption and business outcomes.

Examples include:

### Adoption

- Active users
- Approved use cases
- Usage frequency
- Training completion

### Productivity

- Time saved
- Process cycle time
- Manual effort reduced

### Business Value

- Revenue impact
- Cost reduction
- Customer experience
- Operational efficiency

### Risk

- AI incidents
- Policy exceptions
- High-risk use cases
- Control effectiveness

---

## Executive Perspective

AI adoption should be treated as a business transformation program rather than simply a technology deployment.

The goal is to move from:

**"We have AI tools."**

to:

**"We have measurable business outcomes enabled by AI, supported by appropriate governance and risk management."**

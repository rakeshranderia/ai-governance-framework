# AI Governance Framework

A practical executive framework for responsible AI adoption, governance, risk management and organisational implementation.

## Purpose

This repository explores how organisations can adopt Artificial Intelligence while appropriately managing security, privacy, regulatory, ethical and operational risks.

The objective is to create a governance approach that enables innovation while maintaining appropriate organisational controls.

---

## Focus Areas

- AI Governance
- AI Risk Management
- Responsible AI
- AI Security
- Privacy and Data Governance
- AI Adoption
- AI Assurance
- Executive Oversight

---

## Framework

The approach is based on:

**Identify → Assess → Govern → Implement → Monitor → Improve**

Governance should be proportionate to the risk and potential impact of each AI use case.

---

## Repository Structure

### Governance

### Framework

### Implementation

### Architecture

### Metrics

---

## Executive Perspective

AI governance should not be viewed as a mechanism for preventing AI adoption.

The objective is to create the confidence and controls required to adopt AI responsibly and at scale.

**Innovation + Business Value + Risk + Governance**

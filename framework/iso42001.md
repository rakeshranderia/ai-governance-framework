# ISO/IEC 42001 — Artificial Intelligence Management System

## Overview

ISO/IEC 42001 is an international standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).

It provides organisations with a structured approach to managing the opportunities and risks associated with the use and development of AI.

---

## What is an AIMS?

An Artificial Intelligence Management System provides the governance structure through which an organisation manages AI-related risks and opportunities.

It brings together:

- AI governance
- Risk management
- Policies
- Roles and responsibilities
- AI objectives
- Risk treatment
- Operational controls
- Monitoring
- Continual improvement

---

## Management System Approach

An AIMS should operate as part of the organisation's broader governance and risk environment.

A practical lifecycle is:

**Understand → Assess → Plan → Implement → Measure → Improve**

### Understand

Establish:

- Organisational context
- Interested parties
- AI use cases
- Scope
- Business objectives
- AI-related risks and opportunities

### Assess

Identify and evaluate risks associated with:

- AI systems
- Data
- Security
- Privacy
- Third parties
- Human oversight
- Accuracy
- Bias
- Regulatory requirements

### Plan

Define:

- AI objectives
- Risk treatment
- Policies
- Controls
- Responsibilities
- Monitoring requirements

### Implement

Implement the required:

- Governance processes
- Technical controls
- Policies
- Training
- Monitoring
- Documentation

### Measure

Evaluate:

- Control effectiveness
- AI performance
- Risk indicators
- Incidents
- Objectives
- Audit findings

### Improve

Use findings and lessons learned to continuously improve the AI management system.

---

## AI Governance Controls

An organisation implementing an AIMS should consider controls relating to:

- AI policy
- AI risk assessment
- AI system inventory
- Data management
- Security
- Privacy
- Human oversight
- Transparency
- AI system lifecycle
- Supplier management
- Incident management
- Monitoring
- Documentation
- Continual improvement

---

## AI System Lifecycle

Governance should extend across the lifecycle of an AI system:

**Design → Develop → Test → Approve → Deploy → Monitor → Retire**

Controls and assurance requirements should be proportionate to the risk and impact of the AI system.

---

## Relationship to ISO 27001

ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 27001 can complement each other.

ISO 27001 focuses on managing information security risk, while ISO 42001 provides a management-system approach specifically focused on artificial intelligence.

Where appropriate, organisations can integrate the two management systems.

For example:

**Information Security → Data Protection → AI Security → AI Governance**

---

## Executive Perspective

ISO/IEC 42001 provides a useful framework for moving AI governance from individual policies and projects toward an organisational management system.

The objective should not simply be certification.

The objective should be:

> **A repeatable and measurable approach to managing AI risk while enabling responsible innovation.**

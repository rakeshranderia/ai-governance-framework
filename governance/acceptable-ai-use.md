# Acceptable AI Use

## Purpose

Artificial Intelligence can provide significant benefits to employees and organisations.

This guidance establishes practical expectations for using AI tools responsibly while protecting organisational information, customers, employees and intellectual property.

The objective is to enable appropriate use of AI rather than prevent innovation.

---

## Guiding Principles

Employees using AI should:

- Use AI for legitimate business purposes
- Protect confidential and sensitive information
- Validate important AI-generated information
- Maintain appropriate human oversight
- Follow existing organisational policies
- Report suspected AI-related incidents
- Consider the potential impact of AI-generated content

---

## Appropriate Uses

Examples of generally appropriate uses may include:

- Drafting and improving documents
- Summarising non-sensitive information
- Brainstorming
- Creating presentation outlines
- Developing ideas
- Generating code for review
- Analysing appropriately approved data
- Improving productivity
- Creating training materials

Use should remain subject to organisational security and privacy requirements.

---

## Information That Should Not Be Entered Into Public AI Tools

Unless specifically approved, employees should not enter:

- Confidential business information
- Customer information
- Personal information
- Authentication credentials
- Security information
- Financial information
- Commercially sensitive information
- Proprietary intellectual property
- Unreleased business information

Approved enterprise AI platforms may have different requirements and controls.

---

## Human Oversight

AI-generated content should not automatically be treated as accurate.

Employees should review AI-generated outputs before relying on them, particularly when the output could affect:

- Customers
- Employees
- Financial decisions
- Legal matters
- Security
- Regulatory obligations
- Business-critical processes

---

## Accuracy and Hallucinations

AI systems can produce information that appears credible but is incorrect.

Users should:

- Verify important facts
- Check calculations
- Validate references
- Review generated code
- Confirm regulatory or legal information
- Apply professional judgement

AI should be treated as an assistant rather than an unquestionable source of truth.

---

## Security Considerations

Users should consider:

- What information is being provided to the AI system
- Where the information may be processed
- Who may have access to the information
- Whether the AI provider retains data
- Whether the service has been approved by the organisation

---

## AI-Generated Content

Where appropriate, users should consider whether AI-generated content needs to be disclosed.

Examples may include:

- Customer communications
- Public content
- Marketing material
- Formal reports
- Material decisions

Transparency requirements may vary depending on the context and applicable obligations.

---

## Prohibited Uses

AI should not be used to:

- Circumvent security controls
- Access information without authorisation
- Generate malicious content
- Make unauthorised decisions on behalf of the organisation
- Process sensitive information through unapproved services
- Misrepresent AI-generated material where disclosure is required
- Perform activities that breach organisational policies or applicable law

---

## Reporting

Employees should report:

- Suspected data leakage
- Security incidents
- Unexpected AI behaviour
- Incorrect high-impact outputs
- Suspected misuse
- Unauthorised AI tools
- Privacy concerns

AI-related incidents should be managed through the organisation's existing incident management processes.

---

## Executive Perspective

AI acceptable-use guidance should strike a balance between:

**Enablement + Security + Privacy + Accountability**

The objective should be to provide employees with clear boundaries while allowing them to safely explore and adopt useful AI capabilities.

Good AI governance should answer:

> **"What can I safely do with AI at work?"**
